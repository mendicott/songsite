In the End of Days

key A minor (= books) tempo ≈129 chords Am Dm F

“In the end of days, world in ever change — clouds are hiding sun, night is on the run, till it turns around.” A Shiva prayer-song printed in eight books of the collection, its verses opening into an Om Namah Shivaya refrain. The unilaiva songbook credits it to Amir Paiss of Sheva. The melody here follows the singing on Avalon Pleiades — Om Namah Shivaya / In the End of Days (Heart Feel cover).
