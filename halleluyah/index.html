Halleluyah

key G major on source songbook chords Am F C G Em tempo ≈118

One word of praise, sung over and over until it carries itself — the circles' own halleluyah, printed plain in six books of the collection and not to be confused with any other song of the name. The melody here follows the singing on Ananda Das's rainbow-songs compilation (at 37:07).
