Let Your Little Light Shine

key D minor on source songbook key A minor tempo ≈129 chords Am G C

“Let your little light shine, shine, shine — there might be someone down in the valley trying to get home.” A gospel-rooted circle song, distinct from This Little Light of Mine — printed in Rainbow Caravan, song 56 (p.39, words and chords) and three more books of the collection. The melody here follows the singing on Maxime Polydor — Let your little light shine, rainbow song played on ukulele.
