Let the Way of the Heart

key A minor on source songbook key A minor tempo ≈152 chords Am G C

A circle song of the heart’s way shining through — printed in Rainbow Caravan, song 20 (p.22, words and chords) and Sound of The Heart p.51, and carried in some two dozen of the collection’s songbooks. The melody here follows the singing on Ananda Das — Rainbow Spirit Oregon: Let the Way of the Heart Shine Thru.
