Agua de Estrellas

Limpia, limpia corazón

key F minor on source songbook key A minor tempo ≈112 chords Am C G F Dm Em

A Spanish-language water blessing — its “Limpia limpia, limpia corazón” bridge is how many circles name the song. Printed in Songs of Light p.147 and Mantra-Sammlung Rafael p.72 among roughly fifteen books of the collection; authorship is sometimes credited to Miguel Molina. The melody here follows the singing on The White Arrow — Limpia corazón.
