Music Is the Key

key D minor on source songbook key D minor tempo ≈129 chords Dm C Bb

“And there's a spirit bringing people together — music is the key.” A campfire anthem about the thing the circles are actually doing when they sing. The words and chords are preserved in Dennis's 2021 songbook in D minor, and the melody here follows the singing on Ananda Das's rainbow-songs compilation (at 25:49) — one of the songs rescued from memory by that recording.
