I Find My Joy

In the simple things

key A minor on source songbook key A minor / G / C tempo ≈112 chords Am G C

“I find my joy in the simple things that are coming from the earth” — with its wordless heya-heya chorus. Printed in Alli Ruhi — Songs of the Rainbow p.99 and Sound of The Heart song 131 (p.16), among fifteen books of the collection. The melody here follows the singing on Primitive Tribal Craft — I Find My Joy, Nitsan Charcko Mizrachi.
