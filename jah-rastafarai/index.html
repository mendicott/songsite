Jah Rastafarai

key D minor on source songbook chords Am G tempo ≈152

“Jah Rastafarai, you are my salvation — you help I realize that love surrounding us all, in every situation, here with I and I.” A short reggae-rooted chant printed in eight books of the collection; Sound of The Heart adds Hebrew verses by Shimshay, Zohar and Shimon Lev Tahor. The melody here follows Mettaben's dedicated 2009 recording from the Rainbow Chant Collection (CC BY-NC-SA).
