The Core 50, song by song

The songbooks preserve the words and chords; the melodies are the endangered part. These pages restore verified staff notation, one song at a time. A census across 46 independent songbooks, the community archives, and the recorded evidence confirmed every song in the top 50 — the project’s working focus. 45 of 45 core pages built so far. Songs under active copyright (marked below) are honored by reference only — their notation is available from the rights holders. The source corpus is catalogued in the Rainbow Songbook Collection; how songs qualify and how the fifty were chosen is explained in the method, briefly.
