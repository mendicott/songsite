A World Made of Rainbows

Sacred Rainbow Nation Songs of Light, Peace and Love

Toward a Top 100, song by song

The songbooks preserve the words and chords; the melodies are the endangered part. These pages restore them as staff notation, one song at a time, for the songs sung most widely in Rainbow circles around the world — 64 so far, on the way to a Top 100. The songbooks behind them are gathered in the Rainbow Songbook Collection, and the method, briefly explains how songs are chosen and checked.

Songs are listed by how many of the collection’s songbooks print them, most widely printed first.
