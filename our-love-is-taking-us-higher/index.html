Our Love Is Taking Us Higher

key A major on source songbook key A major tempo ≈123 chords A E D

“Our Love is taking us higher — oh oh oh!” A song of the vision itself: swords becoming plows, crystal cities and golden doors, and the question every land asks — Rainbow People, what have you learned? Printed in the Australian songbook, whose image-only page was read by eye to recover the words. The melody here follows the singing on Ananda Das's rainbow-songs compilation (at 49:45) — in A major, exactly as the book chords it.
