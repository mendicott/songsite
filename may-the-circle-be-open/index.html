Sacred Rainbow Nation Songs of Light, Peace and Love · Song 2

May the Circle Be Open

key B minor on source tempo ≈129 credited to Starhawk

By Starhawk, from The Spiral Dance (1979) — the closing blessing of the circle, sung as gatherings part: “merry meet and merry part and merry meet again.” It is printed in Rainbow AU songs, vol. A, p.1, song #2 — the book’s second song, right where a closing blessing belongs. The melody here follows the singing on the performance carried by song-circle.com’s Merry Meet page, the version the circle-song community itself points to.
