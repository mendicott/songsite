Mother of My Heart

key G major on source songbook key G major tempo ≈92 chords G D C

“Mother of my heart, mother of my soul — sweet mother Mary, mama Gaia, Pacha Mama — she is calling us home.” A mother-song from the medicine valleys printed in ten books of the collection, sung here by Uri (Budgie) in a dedicated recording for the Rainbow Caravan songbook. The melody here follows the singing on Uri (Budgie) — Mother of My Heart, Rainbow Caravan Songbook Collection 1 (song #41; starts at 3:00).
