Rainbow Country

key F major on source tempo ≈144 chords none printed

“We're going to that rainbow country, we're moving to the promised land.” A gathering anthem carried by the voice of Rocker T, recorded live at the Michigan gathering in 2002 and passed around ever since on the Sounds from the Rainbow compilations — it appears on five separate archive.org collections. It is a different song from Bob Marley's “Rainbow Country”, sharing only the title. The melody here follows the Heartsongs Vol. 2 recording (CC BY-NC-SA).
