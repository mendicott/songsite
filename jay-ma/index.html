Jay Ma

key D minor on source songbook key E minor tempo ≈144 chords Em C D

“When the fire in my soul burns a longing for the goal, then I know in my heart it is you — Jay Ma.” A song to the Divine Mother that the circles share with the wider kirtan world, printed with converging E-minor chords in half a dozen books of the collection, from Sound of The Heart to the unilaiva songbook. The melody here follows the singing on Stefan Pulsaris & Teresa — Jai MA (Medicine}SONG Book).
