Celestial Heart

key G major on source songbook chords Em Bm C G D tempo ≈99

“Oh my celestial heart, with your love that takes me so high.” A song by Shimon Lev Tahor — the compiler of Alli Ruhi, Songs of the Rainbow — printed in eleven books of the collection. The melody here follows his own singing, in a live recording of Celestial Heart, and lands in G major, the relative key of the books' E-minor chording.
