key A minor on source songbook key A minor tempo ≈123 chords Am C Em7 B G D

“From the temple of my heart up to the highest mountain, love embrace all that is and all that could be.” A song the circles carried and Kevin James carried onward — printed in eleven books of the collection, with the elah-elah refrain that circles sing long after the verses end. The melody here follows the singing on Kevin James — Temple of My Heart (official video), which lands exactly in the books' A-minor chording.
