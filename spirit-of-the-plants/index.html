Spirit of the Plants

key D minor on source songbook key D minor tempo ≈103 chords Dm Bb Gm Am C

“The spirit of the plants has come to me, in the form of a beautiful dancing green woman.” A medicine-circle song whose verses walk the elements — wind, earth and sea, each a dancing woman of her own color — printed in eleven books of the collection. The melody here follows the singing on Release — Spirit of the Plants, which lands exactly in the books' D-minor chording.
