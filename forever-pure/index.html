Forever Pure

key B minor on source songbook key E minor tempo ≈123 chords Em C D Am

“You are forever pure, you are forever true, and the dream of this world can never touch you.” The English singing of Sudossi Budossi — Madalasa's lullaby from the Markandeya Purana, sung to children who every one became sadhus — and the widest-attested song added since the Core 50, printed in twenty-five books of the collection. The melody here follows the singing on Avalon Pleiades — You Are Forever Pure (Suddhosi Buddhosi).
