Ide Were

key G minor on source songbook key E minor tempo ≈172 chords Em D C G Bm

A traditional Yoruba chant to Ochún, the goddess of love and the fresh waters — it sings of a necklace given as a symbol of initiation into love, an explanation many of the songbooks print beneath the words. It is one of the most widely printed songs in the whole collection, and most books point to the recording by Deva Premal, whose singing the melody here follows: Deva Premal — Idé Weré Weré.
